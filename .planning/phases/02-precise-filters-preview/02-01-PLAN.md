## Phase Goal

**As a** Home Assistant user, **I want to** pinpoint specific shows with field-scoped, Unicode-safe regex plus duration and time-window filters, shape event titles and descriptions with templates, check the matches in a preview before saving, and retune any calendar instantly, **so that** each calendar shows exactly the programmes I care about, clearly titled, without re-downloading the source.

<objective>
Make calendar subentry edits take effect in place: the single diffing update listener detects a changed calendar subentry and hands it to the existing entity, which rebuilds its filter and recomputes matches from coordinator.data. No re-download, no re-parse, no entry reload, siblings untouched (FLT-11 backend, D-04). Also close the Phase 1 carry-over IN-14 (OverflowError near datetime.min in MatchIndex).

Purpose: FLT-11's "retune instantly" is the architectural backbone of this phase. The reconfigure flow (02-05) and every later settings change rely on it.
Output: calendar.py and __init__.py in-place update path, window.py clamp, tests/test_reconfigure.py.
</objective>
