## Phase Goal

**As a** Home Assistant user, **I want to** pinpoint specific shows with field-scoped, Unicode-safe regex plus duration and time-window filters, shape event titles and descriptions with templates, check the matches in a preview before saving, and retune any calendar instantly, **so that** each calendar shows exactly the programmes I care about, clearly titled, without re-downloading the source.

(Story text reused verbatim from 02-01..02-08. The ROADMAP Goal line itself is a capability statement, not a user story; see 02-VERIFICATION.md "Mode".)

Gap closed by this plan: 02-VERIFICATION.md `gaps[0]`, the failed 02-01 truth 6 / T-02-01. Same root cause as review finding CR-01 (02-REVIEW.md).

<objective>
TDD gap closure for CR-01. `re.compile` rejects a pattern with `re.error`, but also with `OverflowError` (repeat count of 2**32 - 1 or more, e.g. `a{99999999999}`, 14 characters, under the 300-character cap) and `RecursionError` (very deep nesting, reachable only through stored data because of that cap). Two places catch only `re.error`:

1. `calendar.py` `_build_spec`: a stored overflow pattern raises out of `XmltvCalendarEntity.__init__` (the calendar platform sets up no entity for the whole source) and out of `async_apply_subentry` (the update listener aborts, no WARNING, sibling calendars are not updated).
2. `xmltv/filter.py` `first_invalid_pattern`: a typed overflow pattern raises out of the calendar form, which the user sees as "Unknown error" instead of `invalid_regex`.

The fix is one shared exception tuple, `PATTERN_ERRORS`, defined next to `compile_patterns` and caught at both sites, plus regression tests at the listener, platform-setup, pure-validation, add-form and edit-form level.

Purpose: 02-01 truth 6 (T-02-01) and FLT-07 must hold for every pattern `re.compile` rejects, not just for syntax errors. No later phase covers this.
Output: `PATTERN_ERRORS` in filter.py, widened catches in `_build_spec` and `first_invalid_pattern`, five new or extended regression tests.
</objective>
