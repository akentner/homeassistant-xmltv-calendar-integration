## Phase Goal

**As a** Home Assistant user, **I want to** add an XMLTV source (URL or local file) in the HA UI and attach calendars filtered by channel and include/exclude regex, **so that** I see exactly the matching programmes as correctly timed calendar events that drive my calendar-trigger automations.

<objective>
Real-world check of the finished slice on the user's local Podman HA instance (D-16), plus the real German feed excerpt (D-15). Claude automates the deployment and the excerpt tooling. The UI walkthrough (sources, calendars, channel picker with a large feed, a firing automation) goes into human-check blocks that the verifier gathers into UAT.md at the end of the phase.

Purpose: the frontend picker with thousands of channels, the real container and a real automation cannot be covered by pytest (RESEARCH "Manual-only" list).
Output: ha-dev running the integration, an approved real-feed excerpt with its test, and UAT checks.
</objective>
