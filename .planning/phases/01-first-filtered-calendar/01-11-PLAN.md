## Phase Goal

**As a** Home Assistant user, **I want to** add an XMLTV source (URL or local file) in the HA UI and attach calendars filtered by channel and include/exclude regex, **so that** I see exactly the matching programmes as correctly timed calendar events that drive my calendar-trigger automations.

Gap closed by this plan: ROADMAP SC3 clause "a single malformed programme never breaks the calendar" (01-VERIFICATION.md `gaps[0]`, review finding CR-01 plus the contributing warning WR-06).

<objective>
TDD gap closure for CR-01: contain per-programme timestamp errors so that one broken `<programme>` in a third-party EPG can never reject the whole feed, crash `parse()`, or crash event building.

Three verified failure paths (01-VERIFICATION.md Behavioral Spot-Checks, re-reproduced while planning):
1. Start offset `+2500` or `-2500`: `_tz()` raises ValueError outside the try, `_STREAM_ERRORS` turns it into `FeedError: unreadable feed: ValueError`, and the valid programmes of the same feed are lost. A stop with offset `+2500` does the same.
2. Year 0001 (+0100) or year 9999 (-0100, or +0000 without stop): OverflowError escapes `normalize_programmes` uncaught.
3. A year-9999 programme that reaches the calendar: `dt_util.as_local` raises OverflowError under Europe/Berlin; `build_events` and `event` catch only HomeAssistantError.

Purpose: SC3 is a roadmap Success Criterion and the contract of 01-02 (truths 1, 4), 01-04 (truths 1, 7) and 01-05 (truth 7). No later phase covers it.
Output: range-validated `parse_xmltv_time`, a per-programme guard plus read-only stream-error translation in the parser, widened guards in the calendar, and regression tests at every layer.
</objective>
