## Phase Goal

**As a** Home Assistant user, **I want to** add an XMLTV source (URL or local file) in the HA UI and attach calendars filtered by channel and include/exclude regex, **so that** I see exactly the matching programmes as correctly timed calendar events that drive my calendar-trigger automations.

Purpose: the core value is "exactly the programmes the user cares about". The truth table is the specification, so it is written first.
Output: filter.py with full semantics, a pure truth-table test and an end-to-end include tracer.
</objective>

    - include "^Der Fall$" gives P3 (sub-title match)
    - include "formel\s*1|formula\s*1" gives P5 and P6
    - include "tatort" with exclude "Der Fall" gives no programme (P3's sub-title and P4's title both match the exclude)
    - include "" with exclude "" gives all 7
    - include "\n  \n" (blank lines only) gives all 7
    - exclude "^Live$" gives everything except P5
    - include "groß" gives P7 (literal umlaut; Unicode normalization is Phase 2)
    - channels ["zdf.de"] gives P3 and P4
    - channel_regex "^sky" gives P5 and P6

    - channels ["gone.de", "zdf.de"] gives P3 and P4 with missing_channels {"gone.de"}
    - channels [] with channel_regex "" gives all channels
    first_invalid_pattern:
    - "ok\n(" gives InvalidPattern(pattern="(", message non-empty); assert the pattern, never the message text
    - "a"*301 gives InvalidPattern
    - 51 non-blank lines gives InvalidPattern
    - "" gives None; "a\n\nb" gives None
  </behavior>
  <implementation>compile_patterns strips each line, skips blank lines and compiles with re.IGNORECASE. first_invalid_pattern enforces MAX_PATTERN_LENGTH and MAX_PATTERN_LINES before compiling. FilterSpec.from_mapping reads KEY_CHANNELS (list), KEY_CHANNEL_REGEX, KEY_INCLUDE and KEY_EXCLUDE (multiline strings, missing means ""). apply evaluates the channel predicate first, then include with any() over the fields (title, then each sub-title), then exclude over the same fields. missing_channels = selected IDs minus epg.channels keys minus channel_ids seen in programmes.</implementation>
</feature>
