## Phase Goal

**As a** Home Assistant user, **I want to** add an XMLTV source (URL or local file) in the HA UI and attach calendars filtered by channel and include/exclude regex, **so that** I see exactly the matching programmes as correctly timed calendar events that drive my calendar-trigger automations.

<objective>
TDD feature: robust feed ingestion. Any payload, whether plain or gzip regardless of its name, large, truncated or partly malformed, becomes either EpgData or a typed, translated failure, and never consumes unbounded memory. This covers both ends of the pipe: the pure parser (gzip sniff, decompressed cap, typed errors, display-name fallback for programme-only channels) and the HA download (streamed with a byte cap and timeouts, host-only logging).

Purpose: SRC-03 and the bounded-memory half of SRC-09. Real feeds are served gzip under .xml names, are tens of MB, and break mid-download.
Output: hardened parser.py, source.py and coordinator.py, synthetic edge-case fixtures (D-15) and tests.
</objective>

  <name>Robust feed ingestion: gzip by content, bounded size, typed failures</name>
  <files>custom_components/xmltv_calendar/xmltv/parser.py, custom_components/xmltv_calendar/source.py, custom_components/xmltv_calendar/coordinator.py, custom_components/xmltv_calendar/translations/en.json, tests/xmltv/test_parser.py, tests/test_ingestion.py, tests/test_source.py, tests/fixtures/edge_cases.xml, tests/fixtures/latin1.xml</files>
  <behavior>
    Parser (pure):
    - gzip.compress(basic.xml) parses to the same EpgData programmes as the plain bytes
    - plain bytes whose would-be filename is "guide.xml.gz" parse fine (no name involved at all)
    - gzip data whose decompressed size exceeds max_bytes=1024 raises FeedTooLargeError
    - the first half of a gzip stream raises FeedError (not EOFError or BadGzipFile)
    - b"" raises FeedError; b"not xml" raises FeedError; truncated XML raises FeedError; str(error) does not contain a sentinel string planted inside the broken feed
    - edge_cases.xml:
      - channel "nodisplay.de" without display-name gives display_name "nodisplay.de"
      - programme on undeclared channel "orphan.de" gives EpgData.channels["orphan.de"] == Channel("orphan.de", "orphan.de")
      - three malformed programmes (no title, no start, start "garbage") are skipped with stats.skipped_malformed == 3
      - umlaut title "Großer Preis" preserved
    - latin1.xml (encoding="ISO-8859-1", display-name "Das Erste Ö", title "Straße"): decoded to the same code points
    - yield_every=250 over 1,000 programmes calls the patched parser time.sleep 4 times; yield_every=0 calls it 0 times
    HA:
    - URL "https://epg.example.com/guide.xml" serving gzip bytes with content-type application/octet-stream: entry LOADED, calendar shows the basic.xml events
    - with source.MAX_DOWNLOAD_BYTES patched to 1024 and a 4096-byte body: entry SETUP_RETRY
    - HTTP 500, TimeoutError and aiohttp.ClientError: entry SETUP_RETRY
    - URL with "?token=s3cret": no log record contains "s3cret" or "/guide.xml"; at least one contains "epg.example.com"
    - after a good first load, a refresh serving truncated gzip leaves coordinator.last_update_success False and coordinator.data the identical previous object
  </behavior>
  <implementation>Per RESEARCH Pattern 5 (lines 300-305) and the parse loop (lines 437-457). parse peeks two bytes, seeks back to 0, wraps a gzip stream in gzip.GzipFile(fileobj=...), and in all cases reads through a small _CappedReader(raw, max_bytes) whose read() raises FeedTooLargeError once the running total passes max_bytes. EOFError, gzip.BadGzipFile, zlib.error and ET.ParseError are translated into FeedError carrying position info only. source.async_fetch_url binds stream = resp.content once, iterates stream.iter_chunked(1 << 16), counts bytes against MAX_DOWNLOAD_BYTES (SourceTooLargeError), and uses DOWNLOAD_TIMEOUT. The coordinator maps FeedTooLargeError and SourceTooLargeError to the translation_key "feed_too_large", other SourceError to "unable_to_fetch" and other FeedError to "invalid_feed".</implementation>
</feature>

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-01-10 | Denial of service | xmltv/parser.py gzip decompression (gzip bomb) | high | mitigate | _CappedReader raises FeedTooLargeError past MAX_DECOMPRESSED_BYTES (1 GiB). Parsing streams through iterparse with root.clear() |
| T-01-11 | Denial of service | Expat entity expansion (billion laughs) | medium | transfer | Python's bundled Expat (2.8.1 in the HA container, ≥2.7.2 required) enforces amplification limits. ElementTree never fetches external entities or DTDs |

| T-01-13 | Denial of service | source.py download (huge or slow body) | high | mitigate | MAX_DOWNLOAD_BYTES 256 MiB streamed count plus ClientTimeout(total=300, sock_read=60). Exceeding either ends in UpdateFailed and setup retry |
| T-01-14 | Information disclosure | source.py and coordinator.py logs | medium | mitigate | Only yarl.URL(url).host is logged. The caplog test asserts a query token and the path are absent |
