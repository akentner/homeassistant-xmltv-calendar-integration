## Phase Goal

**As a** Home Assistant user, **I want to** add an XMLTV source (URL or local file) in the HA UI and attach calendars filtered by channel and include/exclude regex, **so that** I see exactly the matching programmes as correctly timed calendar events that drive my calendar-trigger automations.

<objective>
Make the repository installable through HACS and gated by CI from the first push (DST-01, DST-02), and prove the performance half of SRC-09 with a reproducible 60 MB benchmark.

The user-visible capability this adds: the integration is a valid HACS custom repository with a brand icon, and every push is checked by hassfest, HACS validation, Ruff, the unit suite and the benchmark.

Purpose: catch HACS/hassfest failures and supply-chain drift before the first public push (plan 01-10), and replace "parses fast" with measured numbers.
Output: HACS metadata, brand assets, two SHA-pinned workflows, Renovate config, structure/purity tests, the feed generator and the benchmark test.
</objective>
