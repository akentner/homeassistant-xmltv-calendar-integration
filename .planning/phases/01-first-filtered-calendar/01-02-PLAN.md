## Phase Goal

**As a** Home Assistant user, **I want to** add an XMLTV source (URL or local file) in the HA UI and attach calendars filtered by channel and include/exclude regex, **so that** I see exactly the matching programmes as correctly timed calendar events that drive my calendar-trigger automations.

<objective>
TDD feature: correctly timed, de-duplicated, stably identified programmes. Fill the three pure modules that 01-01 created as contracts (timeparse, normalize, render.uid) so that every XMLTV timestamp variant lands at the right instant, missing or broken stop times are inferred per D-12, exact duplicates collapse (EVT-01), and every event carries a UID that survives refreshes (EVT-06).

Purpose: EVT-05 is the most error-prone part of XMLTV (RESEARCH: a naive strptime turns "202610122030 +0200" into 20:03). Writing the table test first is the cheapest way to get it right.
Output: three pure modules with unit tables plus one end-to-end tracer through the calendar.
</objective>

  <behavior>
    Timestamps (compare in UTC):
    - "20261012203000 +0200" gives 2026-10-12 18:30:00 UTC
    - "202610122030 +0200" (12-digit) gives 18:30:00 UTC, not 20:03
    - "20261012203000 +02:00" and "20261012203000+0200" give 18:30:00 UTC
    - "20261012203000" with no offset gives 20:30:00 UTC (D-14)
    - "20261012203000 Z", "UTC" and "GMT" give 20:30:00 UTC; "BST" gives 19:30 UTC; "CET" gives 19:30 UTC; "CEST" gives 18:30 UTC
    - "20261012203000 XYZ" gives None; "garbage" gives None; "" gives None; None gives None; "20261332000000 +0000" (month 13) gives None
    - DST nights with explicit offsets: "20261025023000 +0200" gives 00:30 UTC and "20261025023000 +0100" gives 01:30 UTC; "20260329030000 +0200" gives 01:00 UTC
    Normalization (default_duration 60 min):
    - A, B, C on channel X with no stops: A ends at B.start, B ends at C.start, C ends at C.start + 60 min; stats.inferred_stops is 3
    - stop equal to start, or earlier than start, is inferred like a missing stop
    - two programmes on X with equal start and different titles: both kept, both end at the next strictly later start
    - exact duplicate (same channel_id, start instant, title): one kept (the first in input order); stats.duplicates is 1
    - same title and start on two different channels: both kept
    - back-to-back (A.stop equals B.start): both kept, unchanged
    - output sorted by (start, channel_id, title); shuffled input gives identical output
    - empty input gives an empty tuple
    UID:
    - event_uid for channel "daserste.de", start 2026-10-13 12:00 +0200, title "Tagesschau" is "daserste.de-20261013T100000Z-" followed by the zero-padded 8-hex crc32 of the UTF-8 title
    - two parses of the same bytes give identical UIDs; the same programmes in reversed file order give identical UIDs
    - same channel and start with different titles give different UIDs
  </behavior>
