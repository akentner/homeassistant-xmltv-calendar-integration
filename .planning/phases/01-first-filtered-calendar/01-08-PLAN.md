## Phase Goal

**As a** Home Assistant user, **I want to** add an XMLTV source (URL or local file) in the HA UI and attach calendars filtered by channel and include/exclude regex, **so that** I see exactly the matching programmes as correctly timed calendar events that drive my calendar-trigger automations.

<objective>
Deliver the full "Add calendar" experience and multiple calendars per source:
- the subentry form gains channel regex, include and exclude
- every pattern is validated in the flow with a visible error
- adding a calendar to an unloaded source aborts cleanly
- several calendars share one source without re-downloads, and calendars can be added and removed live

Purpose: FLT-01 to FLT-04 and FLT-07 are what users touch. The engine from 01-03 is useless until the form can store its inputs safely.
Output: extended CalendarSubentryFlow and translations, plus tests for the form and the multi-calendar lifecycle.
</objective>
