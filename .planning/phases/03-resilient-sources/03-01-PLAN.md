## Phase Goal

**As a** Home Assistant user with XMLTV sources, **I want to** have each source cached across restarts, refreshed on my own schedule and only when the feed changed, tolerant of failures and timezone quirks, editable later, and visible when something goes wrong, **so that** every calendar stays populated at minimal network and CPU cost.

<objective>
TDD feature: the refresh schedule engine. A source refreshes either every N hours or once a day at a fixed time in the HA time zone (SRC-05, D-01, D-02). All time maths (slots, DST, freshness, backoff, the 24 h persistent-failure test) lives in one pure, test-first module, `xmltv/schedule.py`, together with the stored source-options contract (D-06). The coordinator keeps a wall-clock refresh target and turns it into `update_interval` right before Home Assistant arms its timer (RESEARCH Pattern 2). The plan also makes the test suite clock-independent (Wave 0 for 03-02..03-04).

Purpose: the user's feed is generated at 18:00, so "daily at 18:15" is the motivating schedule. Later plans (cache freshness, backoff, Repairs) reuse these pure functions instead of re-deriving time maths.
Output: xmltv/schedule.py with tests, coordinator scheduling, const.py aliases, clock-independent test modules.
</objective>
