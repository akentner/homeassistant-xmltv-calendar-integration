## Phase Goal

**As a** Home Assistant user, **I want to** pinpoint specific shows with field-scoped, Unicode-safe regex plus duration and time-window filters, shape event titles and descriptions with templates, check the matches in a preview before saving, and retune any calendar instantly, **so that** each calendar shows exactly the programmes I care about, clearly titled, without re-downloading the source.

<objective>
Put every Phase 2 setting into the UI: an optional "Advanced options" step between the calendar form and the preview, with validation and translations (D-01, D-02, D-05, D-12, D-13, D-20, D-21). After this plan, everything built in 02-01 to 02-06 is reachable without touching storage. The edit-flow rules (D-03 pre-check from stored values, D-15 Back with advanced values), form-level pattern safety and the end-of-phase UAT walkthrough on ha-dev are owned by 02-08, which runs after this plan.

Purpose: field scope, case, window, weekdays, duration and templates only help users once they can set them in the flow and see the effect in the preview.
Output: async_step_advanced, const.py aliases, translations, flow tests.
</objective>
