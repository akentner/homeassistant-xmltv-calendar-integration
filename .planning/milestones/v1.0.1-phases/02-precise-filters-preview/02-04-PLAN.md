## Phase Goal

**As a** Home Assistant user, **I want to** pinpoint specific shows with field-scoped, Unicode-safe regex plus duration and time-window filters, shape event titles and descriptions with templates, check the matches in a preview before saving, and retune any calendar instantly, **so that** each calendar shows exactly the programmes I care about, clearly titled, without re-downloading the source.

<objective>
TDD feature: restrict a calendar by programme duration (min/max minutes, FLT-08) and by a time-of-day window that may cross midnight, combined with weekdays (FLT-09). Evaluate both in HA's time zone (D-09 to D-14). Pattern validation also gains a nested-repetition guard (Phase 1 review WR-01, Claude's discretion on ReDoS hardening).

Purpose: "the evening films on Fridays" and "no 5-minute trailers" are the common precision needs regex cannot express. The max-duration filter also closes WR-07 (absurd stops).
Output: filter.py duration/window/weekday/ReDoS logic, tz wiring in calendar.py and preview.py, pure and end-to-end tests.
</objective>
