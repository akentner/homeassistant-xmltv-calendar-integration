## Phase Goal

**As a** Home Assistant user with filtered XMLTV calendars, **I want to** automate around matches beyond plain calendar triggers (next-match sensor, new-match event, recent past matches, ready-made blueprint) and install a documented, bilingual, tagged release via HACS, **so that** I can act on the programmes I care about without hand-built polling automations and keep the integration up to date.

<objective>
Keep recent past matches visible (EVT-07, D-14..D-19): ended matches stay in the calendar for the calendar's retention days (default 1, stored key retention_days) after the feed drops them, across restarts, merged with the feed by UID, re-checked against filter edits and dropped when the source's timezone options change. 04-07 adds the setting to the calendar form.

Purpose: "what did I miss yesterday" stays answerable from the calendar, also when the feed only lists today onwards.
Output: retention_days contract, pure retention helpers, retained programmes in the match store, capture/merge in the calendar, diagnostics "retained", tests.
</objective>
