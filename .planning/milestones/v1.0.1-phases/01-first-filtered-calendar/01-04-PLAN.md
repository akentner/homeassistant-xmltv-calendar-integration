## Phase Goal

**As a** Home Assistant user, **I want to** add an XMLTV source (URL or local file) in the HA UI and attach calendars filtered by channel and include/exclude regex, **so that** I see exactly the matching programmes as correctly timed calendar events that drive my calendar-trigger automations.

<objective>
TDD feature: calendar window reads. Replace the linear helpers from 01-01 with a bisect-based MatchIndex, cap and offload event construction, and prove with automation-level tests that calendar triggers (start with offset, end at a window boundary) and live on/off state work (AUT-01, AUT-02).

Purpose: RESEARCH found two non-obvious read semantics. Strict overlap loses end triggers at 15-minute boundaries, and CalendarEvent construction costs about 34 µs each. This plan locks both down with tests.
Output: window.MatchIndex, an updated calendar.py, and HA tests that drive real calendar triggers.
</objective>

<feature>
  <name>Indexed, bounded, end-inclusive calendar reads</name>
  <files>custom_components/xmltv_calendar/xmltv/window.py, custom_components/xmltv_calendar/calendar.py, tests/xmltv/test_window.py, tests/test_calendar.py</files>
  <behavior>
    MatchIndex over programmes A 12:00-14:00, B 14:00-15:00, L 00:00-23:00, all UTC on one day:
    - overlapping(14:00, 15:00) contains A (end equals range start, included) and B
    - overlapping(10:00, 12:00) does not contain A (start equals range end, excluded)
    - overlapping(12:30, 13:00) contains A (spanning)
    - overlapping(22:00, 22:30) contains L (long programme found through the max-duration lookback)
    - an empty MatchIndex gives [] and None
    - current_or_next(12:30) gives A; current_or_next(14:00) gives B (A's stop is not after now); current_or_next(23:30) gives None
    - results are sorted by start
    Calendar:
    - an all-channels calendar over a 6,000-programme day returns 5,000 events from get_events
    - build_events skips a programme whose CalendarEvent construction raises HomeAssistantError and keeps the others
  </behavior>
  <implementation>MatchIndex keeps the programmes in start order, a parallel list of starts, and max_duration = max(stop - start). overlapping bisects from bisect_left(starts, start - max_duration) to bisect_left(starts, end) and keeps those with stop at or after start (RESEARCH Pattern 3). current_or_next scans forward from bisect_left(starts, now - max_duration) to the first programme whose stop is after now. calendar.py stores MatchIndex(result.matches) in _async_refilter, slices overlapping(...) to MAX_EVENTS_PER_QUERY (debug log when truncated), and calls build_events on the loop for up to EXECUTOR_BUILD_THRESHOLD programmes and through hass.async_add_executor_job above that (RESEARCH Pitfall 5). build_events wraps each construction in try/except HomeAssistantError (skip plus debug log, Pitfall 6).</implementation>
</feature>
